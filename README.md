The theme this Week 1 was outside, so I made a garden:

CONTROLS:
   -You can click the crops (carrots, berries, melon)
   -You can click the hoe and water bucket

   -Use the waterbucket to click on the garden plots to water them (turning them into an actual image)
   -Then you can click your crop if you have it and click the plot to grow them
   -Use the hoe to click the grown crop(you can tell if its grown if there is a message in the plot after a few seconds) to harvest the crop
   -You can then sell your crops to the market box for gold (each crop gets a different price)

   -You can switch between buying and selling in the market
   -You can switch between which crop you sell/buy in the market
   -You can input the amount of crops you want to sell/buy

GOAL:
    -Make as much gold as you want
    -Collect as many crops as you want
    -Fill out the checklist at the bottom
        -100 Melons
        -100 Berries
        -100 Carrots
        -1000 gold

How to run:
    -Use the demo link and run it in your browser
    -The project can be ran using html so it should work with just the demo link and the browser
        -JS to make buttons and stuff work
        -CSS to make it look pretty and formatted
        -HTML to run and organize it
